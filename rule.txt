1. Scientific and statistical integrity is the highest priority for this portal. Every result, ranking, observation, and statistical statement must be verified against the correct source file and taxonomic or analytical level. Do not remove, suppress, reorder, relabel, or simplify valid data merely to improve presentation or reduce confusion. Retain valid unresolved assignments and label their resolution explicitly. If a result must be filtered or excluded for a scientifically justified reason, document the rule, rationale, and effect clearly in the portal. Never hardcode conclusions for one dataset; derive them from the loaded report data and preserve the underlying values, statistical context, uncertainty, and limitations.
