Overview

Extracts total nucleic acid (DNA and RNA) from bacteria, yeast, and fungi using magnetic bead capture. Bead beating and a 56 °C lysis step handle tough cell walls.

Sample input
1 mL liquid
~2 mm solid
Elution volume
50–80 µL
Hands-on time
~25 min
Total time
~70 min
Eluate storage
4 °C short
−20 °C long

Before you begin

  1. Mix all buffers and suspensions thoroughly right before use. MB, PW, and LBIII may settle or leave undissolved material. Resuspend them completely so pipetting stays consistent.
  2. For samples high in protein, add Proteinase K during the 56 °C incubation, after LBII has gone in. Prepare it at 5–10 mg/mL and use the validated amount for your sample type.
  3. To isolate DNA only, treat the sample with RNase A. See Notes.
  4. If the LBII bottle looks noticeably low before you open it, do not use it. Contact KreatBio for a replacement.

Method

PHASE 1

Lyse

~40 min

  1. Liquid sample. Transfer 1 mL to a 1.5 mL tube. Centrifuge at max speed (~12,000 × g) for 1 min and discard the supernatant.

    If the pellet is very smallRepeat the transfer and spin 2–3 times to build up more material.

  2. Solid sample. Transfer a small amount (~2 mm, or about 2 loopfuls) to a 1.5 mL tube.

  3. Add HB01 and HB05 beads up to roughly the 250 µL mark. Add 500 µL LBII. Vortex at 3000–4000 rpm for 3 min.

  4. Incubate at 56 °C for 30 min.

    Optional — fungi or tough samplesAdd 5–10 µL Proteinase K before starting the 56 °C incubation.

  5. Centrifuge at max speed for 3–5 min. Transfer 250 µL of the clear supernatant to a new tube.

    Optional — DNA onlyAdd 1 µL of 20 mg/mL RNase A here. See Notes.

  6. Add 250 µL LBI, mix well, and leave for 5 min.

PHASE 2

Bind

~7 min

  1. Add 500 µL BB and 15 µL MB. Mix and leave for 5 min.

    Before you startBring MB to room temperature and mix it well. Cold or settled beads bind poorly.

  2. Place the tube on the magnetic rack for 2 min. Discard the supernatant, then take the tube off the rack.

PHASE 3

Wash

~10 min

  1. Add 700 µL WBIII and mix well. Rack for 1 min. Discard the supernatant.

  2. Add 700 µL WBII and mix well. Rack for 1 min. Discard the supernatant, take the tube off the rack, then repeat this wash once.

  3. Air-dry for 5 min with the lid open.

PHASE 4

Elute

~12 min

  1. Add 50–80 µL EB. Mix and leave for 10 min. Rack the tube, then collect the clear eluate.

  2. Keep the supernatant only and avoid carrying over the black beads. Store at 4 °C short term or −20 °C long term.

Notes

To isolate DNA only, remove RNA with RNase A. Two options work.
  1. During extraction. After Step 5, add 1 µL of 20 mg/mL RNase A. Incubate at 37 °C or room temperature for 30 min, then continue.
  2. After extraction. Add 1 µL to the eluate and leave at room temperature for 15 min.
